Coco's spending wallet pays on its own with x402

With Who pays: Coco on its own, Coco asks the store for a skill, gets a 402 Payment Required, signs the USDC payment with this small wallet and gets the skill. Nobody taps “Confirm”. The x402 facilitator pays the network fee, so Coco needs no SOL. Your wallet (the community treasury) tops it up.

Isla Skills is an app store for robot skills. Robots pay on their own, per use, with x402 on Solana.

PublishDevelopers list a skill with a price per use and a payout wallet.
VoteHouse members vote on which skill Coco learns next.
WorkCoco runs its installed skills as tasks around the house.
PayCoco pays the developer on its own for each use, with x402, from a small daily budget the community treasury (any Solana wallet) tops up. 5% stays with the community.

Prototype for IslandDAO V5. The robot is simulated. With a wallet connected on Solana devnet, payments are real USDC (or SOL) transfers. Without it, they are simulated.
